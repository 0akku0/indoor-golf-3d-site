Simulator Technology

Why our clients choose
Back Nine.

Accuracy

Precision ball-tracking

Dual high-speed infrared cameras capture ball spin, speed, launch angle, and club path — rivalling commercial launch monitors at a fraction of the space.

Projection

4K laser projection

Ultra-short-throw 4K laser projector. No bulb degradation, no colour shift over time. Brilliant even in ambient light.

Software

World courses + coaching

Over 200 world-class courses, driving range modes, shot analysis, and multiplayer — the complete on-course experience indoors.
